Legal & Compliance

Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Effective Date: August 11, 2026 | Last Revised: August 2026 | Application: HDSFD (Hyper-Dynamic Student Focus Dashboard)

1. Overview & Purpose

Welcome to HDSFD (accessible at https://hdsfd.pythonanywhere.com). HDSFD is an open, student-first productivity sanctuary designed to help learners track study sessions, organize assignments, sketch notes, and cultivate academic focus through a digital sanctuary environment.

We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding your personal data. This document outlines our data practices, transparency commitments, and terms governing your use of HDSFD.

2. Information We Collect & How It Is Used

A. User Account Information (When Signed In with Google):

  • Google Profile & Email: When you sign in via Google OAuth2, we receive your email address and public name to personalize your dashboard and authenticate your encrypted database partition.
  • Google Drive Access (drive.file): Used strictly to backup and sync your notes and database snapshot file (Notes.json and data.db) within your private HDSFD Backup folder. HDSFD cannot access any other files on your Google Drive.
  • Google Tasks & Calendar: Used to synchronize your study assignments, deadlines, and class schedules directly with your personal calendar and task lists.

B. Local & Study Data (Guest & Signed-In Modes):

  • Focus Timers & Stats: Chronometer logs, completed Pomodoro sessions, study streaks, and sanctuary tree evolution metrics.
  • Notes & Vector Sketches: Sticky notes, text formatting, and hand-drawn canvas drawings created in Tab 3.

3. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

HDSFD's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • No Advertising or Monetization: User data received through Google APIs is never used for serving advertisements, marketing, or retargeting campaigns.
  • No Data Brokering / Selling: We do not sell, rent, license, or transfer any user data to third-party data brokers or commercial entities.
  • No AI Training on Private Files: Your personal Google Drive files and private notes are never used to train generalized artificial intelligence models.

4. Data Storage, Security & Encryption

HDSFD utilizes industry-standard cryptographic practices:

  • All server communications occur exclusively over encrypted HTTPS / TLS 1.3 connections.
  • OAuth refresh tokens and credentials are encrypted and stored in local server database records with strict session isolation.
  • Guest users enjoy full privacy: all guest data is retained strictly in the user's local browser storage (localStorage) and is never transmitted to any external server.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You retain full ownership and control over your data at all times:

  • Disconnect Account: You can disconnect your Google account at any time in Tab 4 (Settings) or revoke access directly via your Google Account Permissions Manager.
  • Purge Data: Clicking "Clear Chat History" or deleting tasks immediately purges records from the active session. To request a permanent server database wipe, contact us at the email below.

6. Terms of Service

Acceptable Use: HDSFD is provided free of charge for students, educators, and self-directed learners. You agree not to misuse the service, launch automated denial-of-service scripts, or attempt unauthorized access to server infrastructure.

Disclaimer of Warranties: HDSFD is provided on an "as-is" and "as-available" basis without warranties of any kind. While we maintain automated daily backups, users are encouraged to maintain independent records of critical academic schedules.

Open Source: Core client components of HDSFD are open-source and maintained with academic integrity for student learning.
